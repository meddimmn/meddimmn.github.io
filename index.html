Meddi Moumouni

Aerospace engineering student · ISAE-SUPAERO · GNC & Space Mission Analysis

ISAE-SUPAERO, Toulouse, France

Engineering Diploma 2023 – 2027

I’m Meddi Moumouni, an aerospace engineering student at ISAE-SUPAERO in Toulouse, specializing in Guidance, Navigation & Control (GNC), control systems and space mission analysis. I like to bridge control theory with hands-on systems engineering: modeling, simulating and implementing complex orbital missions. My ambition is to contribute to next-generation space programmes as a Mission Analysis or GNC Engineer.

What I’m working on

  • Academic project for the French Space Command (CDE): an operational Rendezvous and Proximity Operations (RPO) maneuver planning tool for GEO and LEO satellites.
  • Graduate coursework with the University of Colorado Boulder (remote): Spacecraft Dynamics and Control Specialization by Pr. Hanspeter Schaub.
  • A physical rover testbed based on Papaya Pathfinder, to build practical mechatronics and GNC skills.

Background

  • Space systems: Space Systems Engineering intern at CNES (Spaceship FR) on an astronaut habitat supervision system; research engineer intern at CNRS-IRAP (planetary science).
  • Analog astronaut: GreenHab Officer of Mars Desert Research Station (MDRS) Crew 311 (The Mars Society, Utah).
  • Space robotics: exchange research student at Keio University, Tokyo, in Genya Ishigami’s Space Robotics Group.
  • Student life: SupaeroMoon Science Team Leader (space robotics for ERC), Supaero Space Section (rocketry for EuRoC), OSE tutoring and outreach.
