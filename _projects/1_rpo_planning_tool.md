Academic project carried out at ISAE-SUPAERO for the **French Space Command (CDE)**.

The goal is to develop an **operational Rendezvous and Proximity Operations (RPO) maneuver planning tool** for satellites in **GEO and LEO**.

- **Domain:** space mechanics, guidance and mission analysis
- **Context:** ISAE-SUPAERO engineering diploma (GNC, Control Theory & Space Systems Design & Operations)
