Developed during my internship as **Planetary Science Research Engineer** at **CNRS - IRAP** (Toulouse, April - September 2025).

**SHAKER** is an **open-source Python framework** (Pandas, NumPy, SQLite) that simulates **rock dissolution**.

During the same internship I observed the mission operations workflows of NASA's **Perseverance** and **Curiosity** Mars rovers (SuperCam/ChemCam).

**Keywords:** Python, planetary science, scientific software
