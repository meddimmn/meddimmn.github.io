**Analog Astronaut & GreenHab Officer**, The Mars Society - Mars Desert Research Station (MDRS), Crew 311, Hanksville, Utah, USA (February - March 2025).

- Operated and maintained the **GreenHab** biological life-support module during a **1-month isolated Martian analog simulation**.
- Conducted experiments for several research centers, including **CNES, ESA, KTH, UCF, CNRS, IRAP and TU Delft**.
- Managed human-factor ethics protocols. The mission research outcomes were presented to the French President and ESA.
