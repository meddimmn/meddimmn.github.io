Research project during my exchange at **Keio University** (Tokyo), in the **Space Robotics Group** of Genya Ishigami's lab (September 2025 - March 2026).

I developed **robotic excavation models for micro-gravity regolith collection on Phobos**, using **Ansys Rocky** (granular simulation) and **MATLAB Simscape** (multibody modeling).

**Keywords:** space robotics, granular media, simulation
